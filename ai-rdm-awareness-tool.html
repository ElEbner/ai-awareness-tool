Awareness tool

Responsible AI Use in Research Data Management

This tool is intended to provide you with advice on responsible practices for processing research data with the help of AI tools, adjusted to your research.


Answer two quick questions about your data types and your intended tasks, and you will be presented with relevant awareness points for the selected data types and use cases, with reference to relevant guidelines from publishers, Norwegian research institutions and the European Commission, as well as to European law and research ethics frameworks.


These recommendations apply mainly to the use of general purpose large language models (LLM), which can process a variety of data formats such as text, audio, images and video. The terms “AI tool” and “AI service” used in these recommendations refers to LLMs and services that incorporate LLMs, such as AI-assisted search engines and software, as well as machine learning tools for speech recognition and transcription.
Note that the data types and tasks presented here are not comprehensive, and do not cover development or training of AI models.
